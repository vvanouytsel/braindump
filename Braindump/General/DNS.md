## The Secret to Keeping Your Children Quiet

Imagine you have three children. Now imagine they are constantly screeching for attention. Surely there have to be solutions for that, right?

Well, yes! There is a device called 'Ipad' or 'tablet' that can help you with these problems. Perfect, just go ahead and install some free games on it and you are done!

Nope, that just makes things worse. Now they are screeching even louder because they are getting advertisements every 3 seconds that they can't get rid off. To be honest I can't blame them. The way these adds are created is just insane, you have to be some kind of mathematician to figure out how to close them.

Luckily there are solutions for everything! Either you whip our your credit card and buy some games, or you use an alternative DNS server that blocks name resolving of these advertisement domains. It is really that simple.

 - [Controld](https://controld.com/free-dns) can be used. Just set it as your DNS server on your tablet device and adds will not show up.
   `e.g.: p2.freedns.controld.com`
